## The bot is an AI and it is wrong a lot

Replies are generated by large language models. They are frequently
inaccurate, occasionally confidently fabricated, and never reviewed by a
human before they are sent. The bot's "memories" are LLM-extracted
summaries of past conversation and can be distorted or simply wrong.

Nothing the bot says is advice — not medical, legal, financial,
psychological, or safety advice. Do not act on it. Do not treat its
persona as a person.
